Navigating to the Games page


Landing Page


Figure 1: Our landing page. Here, multiple options for interacting with the site can be selected. Circled in red is the button labeled “Games.” Clicking this button will take you to the Games page.


To access the Games page:

  1. Move your cursor over the blue tab labeled “Games”, labeled in the image above.
  2. Use your left mouse button to click on the word "Games".


Games Page


Figure 2: The Games page. In the center is the label showing Games, with two buttons below it. The button labeled "Matching Game" will take you to a science-based matching game on click. The button labeled "Bingo Game" will take you to a math-based bingo game on click.


To progress to either game:

  1. Move your cursor over the blue tab labeled with your game of choice.
  2. Use the left click function on your mouse to interact with the button.

Find about both games on their own Wiki page.
