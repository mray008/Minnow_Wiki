Navigating to the Login Page


Figure 1: this is our landing page, here you can find all sorts of buttons to navigate to different pages of the Minnow site. Along the top of the page you will find a dark blue bar with several buttons across it each labeled with white plain text, this is called the navigation bar. Note that the button we will be using is circled in red in the screenshot for your convience


To access the Login Page:

  1. Bring your cursor over the button with the word "Login" on it in the navigation bar.
  2. Use the left click function on your mouse to interact with the button.


A Quick Look at the Login Page

Extended annotation for the visually impaired


Figure 2: This is the login page. It is a white rectangle with the text "Login" spanning the top of the rectangle in black text. Below, there are two text boxes labels internally with grey text labeled "Username" and "Password" in that order. Below the text boxes is a dark grey button labeled with white text which says "Login".


Entering the Username


Figure 3: To login we must first provide our username as shown above.


To enter your username:

  1. Bring your cursor over the text box labeled "Username"
  2. Left click on the box and you should see a flashing black bar, this indicates that you are interacting with the text box.
  3. You can begin to type your username useing the keys on your keyboard! You will see your username show in the box in real time.


Entering the Password


Figure 4: Next we need to provide our password, this is similar to the previous step with one key distinction you will not. be able to see your text show in the box in real time, instead, for privacy, your keystrokes will visually add dots to cover your password. Don't worry! Your password will still be entered as typed, it is just visually protected from overlookers.


To enter your password:

  1. Bring your cursor over the text box labeled "Password"
  2. Left click on the box and you should see a flashing black bar, this indicates that you are interacting with the text box.
  3. You can begin to type your password useing the keys on your keyboard! You will see dots in place of the characters you type in the box in real time.


Logging in


Figure 5: Finally, we just need to submit our inputted data and we will be logged in! Above you can see the dark grey login button circled in red.


To log in:

  1. Bring your cursor over the dark grey button labeled "Login"
  2. Use the left click function on your mouse to interact with the button.
