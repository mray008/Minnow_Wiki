Navigating to the Login Page


Show above in the screenshot is our landing page, here you can find all sorts of buttons to navigate to different pages of the Minnow site. To access the MinnowChat, however, we don't need to navigate away from this page! As circled in red in the screenshot above, in the bottom right of the Landing page you will find a round blue button with the text 'Chat with Minnow' on it in plain white text. To open the MinnowChat, bring your cursor over the button and left click on your mouse to interact with the button.
