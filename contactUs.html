Navigating to the Contact Us Page


Landing Page


Figure 1: Our landing page. Here, multiple options for interacting with the site can be selected. Circled in red is a button labeled "Contact Us." There, you can move forward to a page that allows you to contact Team Minnow!


To access the Contact Us page:

  1. Move your cursor over the blue tab labeled “Contact Us.”
  2. Use the left click function on your mouse to interact with the button.


Using the Contact Us feature


Typing in the Message Box


Figure 2: Our Contact Us page. Here, you can see a lable at the top reading "Contact Us," as well as instructions on how to do so below. You can see three white text boxes. The first, labeled "Your Name," is where you can sign your name, so we know who we're talking to! The second, labeled "Your Email," is where you can put an email you like to use, so we know how to get back to you! And finally, the third, labeled "Your Message," is where you can write whatever comment, question, or concern you have to share with us!


To use the Contact Us feature:

  1. Move your cursor over the white tab labeled "Your Name."
  2. Use the left click function on your mouse to interact with the button.
  3. Write your name, or what you'd like us to call you when we respond.
  4. Move your cursor over the white tab labeled "Your Email."
  5. Use the left click function on your mouse to interact with the button.
  6. Write the email that you'd like us to send our reply to.
  7. Move your cursor over the white tab labeled "Your Message."
  8. Use the left click function on your mouse to interact with the button.
  9. Write your message!
  10. Move your cursor over the gray button below that labeled "Send Message."
  11. Use the left click function on your mouse to interact with the button.
  12. Congratulations! Your message has been sent!


After the Message is Sent


Figure 3: After you select the option to send your message to us, you will see this screen thanking you for reaching out. Congratulations! This means your message has gone through, and we will be happy to respond shortly!
