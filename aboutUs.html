Navigating to About Us


Landing Page


Figure 1: Back at the Landing page, you can see the fourth option in the menu, “About Us”. This page contains information and pictures of the team that put the Minnow project together! By clikking on the "About Us" button, (Circled in red), you will be taken to the About Us page.


The Team behind Minnow

Say hello to the programmers behind this product


Figure 2: You have reached the About Us page! Here, you can see the pictures and names of the teammembers who developed Minnow, one person at at time. Also, there is a brief description of their character and/or hobbies, so that you can get to know them better!


Moving through the teammembers


Using the "right" option


Figure 3: You may recall that this page has teammembers, not just one. To view the next teammeber, click on the big seashell on the right side of the picture (it is circled in red). This will take you to the next teammeber! (There are six in total).


If you want to go the other way...


Figure 4: You don't have to go to right all the time. In fact, if you prefer, you don't have to at all! If you prefer going left or you want to go back to a person you already viewed, just click on the big seashell to the left of the picture (whoever it is). Also, there is no limit to going right or left.


If you need to go down

A reminder that you may not see the full view


Figure 5: You might encounter a picture that is too big for your screen, or you can't see the message. No problem! Just scroll down or use the vertical scroll bar. (See the red arrow) (Note: depending on your system, the bar may have a diferent appearance, and you may have to move your mouse over it to make it appear.)
