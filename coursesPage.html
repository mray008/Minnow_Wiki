Navigating to Courses


Landing Page


Figure 1: Our landing page, with multiple options for navigating the Minnow platform. Circled in red is the Courses button, which when clicked takes you to the Courses page.


To navigate to the Courses page:

  1. Bring your cursor over white text that says "Courses", labeled in the image above.
  2. Use your left mouse button to click on the word "Courses".


Courses Page


Figure 2: Our Courses page. Two squares labeled Math and Science take up the majority of the space. Hovering the cursor over each causes them to move slightly upward, indicating that they are selected and clickable. Within each box is a small description of each subject that is provided by Minnow, giving an overview of what you will be learning and doing.



To head to a course:

  1. Move your cursor over one of the courses, causing it to move up.
  2. Use your left mouse button and click on the course your cursor is over.
