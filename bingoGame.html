To Create the Game:

  1. 1. Select the operation that you would like to practice on.
  2. 2. Select the difficulty you would like to play one.

Figure 1.2: Before Start Game


Once the game has been started you will see the computer's board generated and ready to go. This will also cause the timer to start.If you get questions wrong it will penalize your score, but getting questions right will increase your score depending on the difficulty of the question. During the computer's turn you will see an option to steal the computer's turn. This will allow you to get additional points to your score. These stolen points will be worth more than normal questions. Stealing the computer's turn will also prevent the computer from marking their board as well. If you get the stolen question incorrect though it will deduct your points by 50.

  1. 1. Click the start game section.
  2. 2. After start game section has been clicked, the computer's board will be generated.
